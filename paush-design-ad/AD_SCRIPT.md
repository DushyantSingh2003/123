## Why this order works

- **0–1 s:** before/after slider (empty grey room vs styled room). It's the most-stopped visual in interior ads and shows the result before any talking.
- **The name card** builds a personal brand: a real designer, a real city.
- **Residential + commercial** in 4 s shows the full range.
- **"Aapki style kaunsi hai?"** turns viewers into participants. The 1–5 numbering plus "Comment karein" drives comments, which helps Meta's delivery.
- **2D → 3D** demonstrates the service (planning and visualization) instead of just claiming it.
- **Luxury vs affordable** removes the "she must be expensive" objection.
- **The call to action** is a single clear action: message.
