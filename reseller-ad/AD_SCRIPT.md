# WhatsApp Reseller Meta Ad: creative script

**Format:** 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · ~38 s (preview; the voiced cut lands at ~38–40 s depending on the final voice timing) · Reels / Facebook Reels / Meta Ads
**Goal:** a household woman finishes the video thinking *"Main bhi WhatsApp se kapde bech kar ghar se business shuru kar sakti hoon"* and taps the WhatsApp button.
**Order of the pitch:** business opportunity first, products second.

---

## 1. Hook selection

| # | Hook | Why it was / wasn't picked |
|---|------|---------------------------|
| A | **"Roz WhatsApp Status lagati ho na? Good morning wale... tyohaar wale..."** | **Picked.** It's about *her* daily habit, not a product, so it doesn't feel like an ad. Every Indian woman recognises the good-morning/festival status, so she nods along. Then it flips: *"usi Status se kapde bikne lagein toh?"* |
| B | "Bina kurti khareede, kurti bech sakti ho" | Strong curiosity gap, but it sounds like a trick or an MLM line. Less trust. |
| C | "Dukaan kholne ke liye lakhs chahiye... WhatsApp Status free hai" | Clear, but it opens on money/investment, which feels like a pitch from the first second. |

**First frame (thumb-stop):** a full-screen WhatsApp *Status viewer* (progress bars, "My status", "Seen by 48") with a bright Good Morning status and the big hook text **"Roz WhatsApp Status lagati ho? 👀"**. The text is on screen from frame 1, so it works with the sound off.

Captions are kept short and only appear where the big on-screen text doesn't already say the line. Key text stays inside Meta's Reels safe zone (y ≈ 270–1250 px).

**Audio:** voiceover on top. Original synthesised Indo-pop music (tanpura drone, tabla-style hits, plucked melody, 104 BPM) is ducked under the voice. Small sound effects mark each beat: notification ding, whooshes, message pops, taps, stamp thumps, chimes. The music and effects are generated in code, so there are no licensing issues for paid ads.

## 5. Things to check before going live

- **Photo 2 (beige floral shirt set) carries a "RAJRISHI" watermark.** If that isn't your brand, swap the photo before running paid ads (replace `assets/p2_beige_floral_shirt_set.jpg` and re-render).
- **No prices are shown** because none were provided. To add them, give me the exact wholesale prices.
- **Exchange policy and delivery charges are not mentioned** because they weren't confirmed. Tell me if they apply and I'll add a line or chip.
- The chats, names (Neha, Sunita Aunty, Pooja) and "Seen by" counts are **illustrative UI**, not testimonials.
- The voiceover was made on a HeyGen free plan. Confirm your HeyGen plan allows commercial use of the audio in paid ads.
